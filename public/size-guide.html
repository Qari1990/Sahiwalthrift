Size guide

International brands size differently, so please compare measurements.

How to measure

Lay a shirt or jacket that fits you well flat on the floor. Chest: straight across from armpit to armpit. Length: from the highest point of the shoulder to the bottom hem. Compare these with the measurements on the item page.
