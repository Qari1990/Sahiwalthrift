How the Website Grand Launch offer works

  1. Valid on website orders from 23 Nov to 29 Nov, 11:59 PM (Pakistan time). After that the gifts switch off automatically.
  2. Gents jackets: men's or unisex jackets, puffers and coats priced Rs 1,999 or more. Free gift: one gents sweatshirt (Rs 599 piece).
  3. Ladies jackets: women's jackets, puffers and coats priced Rs 1,199 or more. Free gift: one kids sweatshirt (Rs 399 piece).
  4. Qualifying jackets are marked “Free gift inside”. One free gift per order, while stock lasts.
  5. After you add a jacket, your bag shows the free gifts you can pick. Tap “Add free” on the one you want (with a gents jacket you can also pick a kids sweatshirt).
  6. Delivery is free on every order with a qualifying jacket. Cash on delivery all over Pakistan.
  7. Gifts are real one-off pieces from our stock. If the gift you want sells out, pick another one; if none are left, you still get free delivery.
  8. The free gift replaces the bundle discount and the welcome voucher on that order (it is worth more).
  9. Returns: 7 days as usual. If you return the jacket, please return the free gift with it.
