How the Website Grand Launch offer works

  1. Valid on website orders from 23 Nov to 29 Nov, 11:59 PM (Pakistan time). After that the gifts switch off automatically.
  2. Qualifying jackets: men's or unisex jackets, puffers and coats priced Rs 1,999 or more (marked “Free gift inside”).
  3. Buy 1 qualifying jacket: one kids sweatshirt free. Buy 2 or more: one men's sweatshirt free instead. One free gift per order.
  4. After you add a jacket, your bag shows the free gifts you can pick. Tap “Add free” on the one you want (with 2 jackets you can pick a men's or a kids sweatshirt).
  5. Delivery is free on every order with a qualifying jacket. Cash on delivery all over Pakistan.
  6. Gifts are real one-off pieces from our stock. If the gift you want sells out, pick another one; if none are left, you still get free delivery.
  7. The free gift replaces the bundle discount and the welcome voucher on that order (it is worth more).
  8. Returns: 7 days as usual. If you return the jacket, please return the free gift with it.
