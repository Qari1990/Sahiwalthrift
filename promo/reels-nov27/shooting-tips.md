# Agli video shoot: checklist (27 Nov feedback)

## Jo achha hai (yehi rakhein)
- Vertical video, 1 item per video, daylight: sab theek.
- Hanger par ghoomta hua garment: front + back dono dikhte hain. Buyer ko trust milta hai.

## Jo kamzor hai
1. Background: grey tarp (daagh wali), rassi, ladder, wires, deewar ka kona. Kapra nahi, ganda background pehle nazar aata hai.
2. Shuru ke 3-4 second mein garment side se ghoom raha hota hai. Ad mein pehle 2 second mein hi front + brand nazar aana chahiye, warna log scroll kar dete hain.
3. Brand tag close-up nahi hai. "Original brand" ka sab se bara saboot tag hai.
4. Hangers mix hain (purple, grey, white plastic).
5. Dark kapre (black softshell, vest) grey background par dull lagte hain.
6. Video 720p hai.

## Agli shoot (10 minute setup)
- Background: tarp ke aage ek saada safed/off-white chadar ya parda kas kar lagayen (silwat kam). Ya saaf deewar ke aage rassi bandhein. Ladder aur wires frame se bahar.
- Rassi ki jagah ek seedhi rod / pipe lagayen. Tarp nahi, sirf rod + kapra.
- Sab hangers ek jaise: ek hi rang ke wooden ya black hangers (10-12 kaafi hain).
- Light: subah 9-11 ya asar ke waqt, saaya mein (direct dhoop nahi). Phone ka rukh roshni ki taraf ho, kapre ke peeche se nahi.
- Camera: settings mein 1080p 30fps. Phone khara (vertical), kapre se 1.5-2 meter, aankh ki height par. Lens saaf kar lein.
- Har item ki video (8-12 sec):
  1. 0-2 sec: FRONT seedha camera ki taraf (ghumana nahi, haath se pakar kar seedha rakhein).
  2. 2-5 sec: aahista ghumayen, back dikhayen.
  3. 5-8 sec: phone qareeb le ja kar BRAND TAG + size label close-up (2 sec ruk kar, focus ke liye tap karein).
  4. Optional: zipper/fabric/lining close-up 1-2 sec.
- Black/dark kapre: light background ke aage hi shoot karein.
- Rack video (photo wali setup): sab kapre ek taraf face karein, hangers barabar faasle par, phone ko rail ke saath aahista chalayen (left se right), har kapre par 1 sec. Pehle sab se acha/bright piece aage rakhein (abhi grey coat aage hai, puffer/brand wala aage rakhein).

## Editing (hum kar denge)
Aap sirf raw video bhejein. Hum trim, color, brand/size/price label, WhatsApp end card laga dein ge (jaise reels 148-152).
